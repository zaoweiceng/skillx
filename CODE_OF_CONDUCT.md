# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Discuss ideas and code on their merits;
make feedback specific and constructive. Welcome people with different backgrounds
and experience levels.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, and publication
of another person's private information are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when needed to
protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate to the behavior and
apply consistently, including to maintainers.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately through a
contact method they publish on their GitHub profile. If no private channel is
available, request one without naming affected people or posting sensitive details.
Do not use public issues to publish private allegations or personal information.
